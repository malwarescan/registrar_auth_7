Find expired domains that match what you are building

Describe your business, product or idea in a sentence. The finder picks out the key terms, searches current NameSilo expired-domain auctions for names that contain them, and ranks the matches with a fit score, the recorded bid, the end time and a link to the auction. This search needs JavaScript; the guides and data below work without it.

How to use the domain finder

  1. Type a short brief, for example "AI receptionist for dental clinics" or "local pizza delivery".
  2. Check the terms the finder detected and edit the brief if it missed what matters.
  3. Compare the ranked names: each shows its fit score, recorded bid, auction end time and the tradeoff to watch.
  4. Open a name to see its full score breakdown, then follow the link to bid on NameSilo. Bids are placed on NameSilo, not here.

What the scores mean

Fit, brandability, pronounceability, TLD trust and risk are calculated by fixed, published rules. Read the scoring methodology before relying on a score.
